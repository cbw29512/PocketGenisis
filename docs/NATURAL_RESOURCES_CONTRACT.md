## Mechanical progression
Primitive gather surface wood/stone/clay; learned tools allow efficient cutting/quarrying; actual exploration and prospecting reveal local deposits; knowledge/equipment/labor and safe access permit physical extraction; transport and storage bridge deposits and settlements. Raw ore is not refined metal. Smelting/refining consumes actual ore, fuel, suitable equipment and labor; alloying consumes constituent materials (copper+tin for bronze); metals support stronger tools/infrastructure. Gold/silver/gems can motivate trade, jewelry, culture and later specialized technical uses; no automatic monetary value or global buff. Trade and recycling transfer actual material, loss accounted; extraction can exhaust a site. No teleporting resources, free regeneration, fake discoveries or universal scripted era dates. Rare seams arise from seeded geography, not a loot timer or pity guarantee. Multi-mineral geology, shafts, accidents and industrial processes staged as technology advances.
