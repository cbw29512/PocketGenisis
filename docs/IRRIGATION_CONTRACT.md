# Disposable irrigation increment
The isolated PG_IRRIGATION_PROBE extends agriculture with Irrigation research (requires farming, costs 3 wood/2 stone/2 food, 30% base success) and one shared construction blueprint (4 wood/3 stone, 30 work). A knowledgeable available founder can establish a channel on an unoccupied tile cardinal-adjacent to a completed prepared field, only while field water is below 24 and source tile water exceeds 24. Existing material, work and occupancy rules apply. No artificial source, automatic build or forced discovery. This represents a simple short local water conveyance; terrain elevation, rivers and network routing are not yet implemented.
After each successful real agriculture tick, a completed adjacent channel may transfer at most two existing water units from its own tile to the field, on 60-tick boundaries, leaving at least 24 units in the source and filling only to 24 in the field. No transfer to unfinished/invalid/nonadjacent structures, no transfer at tick zero, no repeated transfer in a tick. Existing natural water renewal and human consumption remain unchanged. Transfer is not new water production and must not increment Production.water. Counts cumulative transfers and units, last successful transfer tick. No fuel, pumps or separate per-citizen scan. It benefits existing farming by restoring actual water; seed, work and seasonal requirements remain unchanged. Numerical thresholds provisional. Zero channels/transfers in a plentiful unforced world is a legitimate result, not a failed civilization.
Tests: knowledge and adjacency prerequisites, actual construction/material debit, reserve/capacity/conservation, no repeated transfer, dry field restored via real tick, no extra production, one-year integrated unforced observation. Separate Elecrow probe extends agriculture. No canonical save, rendering or Tab5 changes. Future expansion requires terrain-derived sources, multi-field networks, maintenance and crop-failure rules.
