# MVP v0.1 — Definition of Done
## Mission
Two immortal founders establish a procedurally generated resource-rich world. All descendants are mortal individuals with distinct personalities, relationships, knowledge, and one primary adult purpose. Birth, childbirth complications, accidents, disease, aging and death matter. Citizens may succeed spectacularly or fail. Founders may be incapacitated but recover; they can repopulate after collapse. Fictional inheritance introduces diversity without human inbreeding modeling. Civilization progresses autonomously, may regress, and has no victory screen. Target five years of operation before hardware migration, with open-ended continuity.
## Scope
Top-down pixel-art map, visible agents, terrain, shelters, weather, day/night, renewable food, wood and water; stone and mineral deposits with future extraction/recycling. Natural resources initially plentiful. Citizen inspection, historical journal and family tree. Initial technology: fire, stone tools, shelter, cooking, storage, farming, irrigation, pottery, sanitation, teaching and records. Experiments use effort, time, materials and prerequisites; success is not guaranteed. Knowledge can be taught, recorded or lost. Future industrial and space ages, diplomacy and advanced economies are roadmap.
## Proposed tuning
12 real hours per simulated year; four 3-hour seasons; independent visible day/night animation labeled as such. Target initial lifespan 60–80 simulated years, gestation 4.5 real hours, adulthood 9 real days. 100x60 terrain cells at assumed 800x480 display; target 256 active citizens subject to benchmarking. Fixed primary purpose after maturity; survival and emergency actions permitted. Capacity pressure defers births, never silently deletes citizens.
## Acceptance gates
1. New world explicitly initialized with exactly two permanent founders; same world, identities, RNG and history restored after reboot. Invalid save never silently creates a world.
2. Seeded host tests demonstrate gathering, shelter, fire experiments, birth, maturation, roles, death, learning, knowledge loss and founder survival/recovery. At least one tested recovery scenario restores a viable population after collapse.
3. Seasonal weather changes growth, water, work and health. No negative resource balances; depleted renewable resources can recover; founder survival and recovery cannot permanently deadlock due to exhausted resources.
4. Display animates agents and environment; touch inspection shows individual identity, age, purpose, traits, family and accomplishments without pausing simulation.
5. Versioned portable export/import preserves world identity, RNG, clock, genealogy, history, technology and state; recover from truncated/corrupt checkpoint or event log; cross-device restore tested on host.
6. Deterministic replay and five real years equivalent accelerated simulation pass invariants; report memory, storage and runtime. This is not physical five-year proof.
7. Exact device passes 72-hour unattended soak with memory, latency, frame rate, temperature, storage-write and power interruption evidence using a disposable test world; supported capacity set from results.
8. Update/recovery procedure tested without replacing the world; original firmware backed up where supported before first flash.
## Boundaries
Offline core first. Streaming is an optional independent extension, not a prerequisite for survival. No external AI, cloud dependence or unlimited-memory promise. Numeric proposals require owner review and benchmarking.

## Development worlds and official launch
Owner decision: finish and validate the autonomous civilization before implementing livestreaming. Development worlds are disposable and may be explicitly restarted while tuning rules, balancing resources, and testing failures. Test worlds must be clearly labeled and isolated from the official world.
Before official launch, freeze a versioned foundational ruleset, pass the release gates, review the hardware results with the owner, and obtain explicit approval to initialize the official world with two immortal founders. Record its creation date and world ID. Thereafter, normal boot, firmware updates, migrations, and civilization collapse must preserve that world; never silently reset it. Destructive development resets must not target an official save. Future rule changes require versioned migration and regression tests. Livestream implementation is deferred until the civilization is ready and the owner revisits the feature.

## Foundational law: independent seeded emergence (owner-confirmed central idea)
Two identical devices running the same rules with distinct seeds should develop substantially different civilizations through local interactions, not scripted narratives or seed-selected future storylines; a single seed-selected founding event is permitted as an initial condition. A seed determines procedural terrain, founder variation, individual decisions and environmental events through documented deterministic random streams. Entropy causes deterioration and loss; maintenance, experimentation, teaching and ecological regeneration counteract it. No forced era transitions, fixed plot, hidden success correction or guaranteed invention dates. The founder recovery and renewable-resource rules apply equally to every seed.
### Release gate — divergence and reproducibility
- Same seed, rules, initial settings and inputs: canonical simulation state and event history must match at each comparison checkpoint across repeated runs and save/reload; exclude external metadata such as creation UTC and device/world UUID from comparison.
- Different seeds: run a proposed fixed suite of at least 32 seeds, with both procedural maps and a controlled same-map cohort. Sample population trajectories, survival, invention order and timing, settlement layout, resource use, and historical outcomes. Publish trajectories, distributions, and representative histories; names, cosmetic differences or terrain alone do not count as meaningful divergence.
- Agree quantitative divergence thresholds and balancing bounds before running the release suite; do not tune to a single favored seed. Record invariant failures, stalled worlds and recovery behavior. Not every pair must diverge dramatically, and convergence is allowed when local conditions favor similar outcomes.
- Compare controlled perturbations to one event or founder trait while holding other initial conditions fixed. Ensure resulting differences arise from state transitions and not artificial divergence multipliers. No external clock, renderer or observer input may change the simulation outcome at equal ticks.

## Owner-approved resilience and wonders
Civilization progress reduces preventable event risk and consequences through actual maintained capabilities, not an era-based global luck multiplier. Clean water and sanitation reduce disease exposure; food reserves and irrigation mitigate famine; sound construction and maintenance reduce structural losses; care and medical knowledge improve recovery and childbirth outcomes. Population density, resource pressure, neglected maintenance and knowledge loss can increase risks again. Exogenous natural hazards retain their own environmental probabilities; preparedness changes exposure and damage, not whether an earthquake occurs. No zero-risk guarantee, scripted catastrophe, forced progress or hidden rescue.
Exceptional world-specific wonders may emerge autonomously from creative citizens, learned techniques, suitable terrain, available surplus, labor and sustained construction. Examples: monumental stone circles, terraced gardens, bridges, towers, great archives or unique founder memorials. Seeded proposals, local constraints and optional failure determine what is attempted; no fixed list of mandatory wonders, free spawning, guaranteed completion or global bonus for mere completion. Each built wonder has a unique identity, location, contributors, construction history, condition and potential functional benefits derived from existing mechanics. Natural landmarks may be discovered and named as separate geographic milestones; not buildings. Ruins and abandoned projects remain meaningful history.
Initial release scope: verify capability-based mitigation for MVP disease/famine/weather impacts and support a minimal exceptional structure through the shared construction and history primitives after ordinary construction works. Additional wonder families, complex disasters and advanced infrastructure are extensions, not prerequisites. Test controlled risk comparisons, loss of protection, resource conservation, deterministic proposal/replay, unique milestones and no mandatory wonder in a world.

## Encounter rarity and pacing (owner intent; provisional tuning)
Rare encounters are genuinely optional: a world can have none of a particular type throughout its observed lifetime, or several when conditions and seed favor them. No pity timer, minimum ongoing rare-event quota, forced later legendary encounter, seed reroll or hidden progress rescue. The single founding event described below is the explicit initialization-only exception. Ordinary births, work, discoveries, art, construction and meaningful historical milestones provide everyday history independently of exceptional encounters. Do not fabricate milestones for a quiet world.
Superseded earlier short-interval rarity proposals: balance exceptional encounters for decades of real operation, using simulation ticks only. Under the provisional 12-real-hour simulated year, candidate aggregate mean intervals under reference eligible conditions: rare 1–6 real months (~60–365 simulated years), epic 1–3 real years (~730–2190 simulated years), legendary 5–20 real years (~3650–14600 simulated years). Specific extraordinary outcomes may have mean waiting times of 20–50+ real years equivalent, conditional on eligibility. Uncommon and ordinary activity may occur much more often. These are unapproved balancing proposals, not calibrated rates, deadlines or a promise of catalog completion; resource locations and genuine achievement rarity are separate from timed encounter frequency. Actual realized rates vary with ecology, exploration, knowledge and eligibility. Common historical activity is not randomly scheduled to meet these targets.
Evaluate seeded exceptional encounter opportunities on a fixed simulation-time cadence, not per renderer frame, citizen, tile or action. Bound aggregate world-level draw opportunities so population growth cannot multiply rare outcomes without limit. Use stable eligible candidate ordering and contextual weights. Retain 90 simulated days as an initial lower-bound pacing fixture, not final pacing; test longer tick-based separation and aggregate opportunity budgets against decades-scale targets and short-window clustering. This is an explicit versioned opportunity constraint, not a suppression of actual discoveries, linked consequences, biological events, naturally occurring hazards or simultaneous achievements. No retroactive removal or downgrading of events. Hidden physical deposits are generated by geography/seed and discovered through exploration; multiple genuine discoveries may cluster. Wonders and artistic/scientific achievements are earned, not random loot.
Evaluate quiet periods, clustering, median/percentile waiting times, zero-event worlds, event counts by tier and exposure across at least 32 seeds for the full simulation and a larger isolated scheduler sample. Include 5 simulated-day, 5 simulated-year, 1/5/10/20/30/50 real-year-equivalent and long-horizon windows, population scaling, save/reload and controlled eligibility. Tune and obtain owner approval before freezing numeric thresholds. Preserve same-seed reproducibility. Minimal cultural artifacts/unique discoveries/rare materials should reuse existing creation, exploration, resource, knowledge and journal primitives; richer procedural art/music and expanded encounter catalogs remain staged enhancements, not separate MVP engines.

## Founding event and causal discovery network (owner-directed)
Exactly one seed-selected formative event on explicit new-world creation at tick zero, independent of ordinary encounter rarity budgets. It establishes an actual world condition, resource, artifact, observation or limited piece of knowledge. Use a versioned weighted catalog of eligible initial effects, deterministic isolated RNG, stable ordering and a universally eligible fallback catalog entry; no retrying a seed for a preferred outcome, no guaranteed power or future storyline. Record immutable origin ID, event ID, affected entities and effects; apply atomically once. Reboot, save import, update, migration and total mortal-population collapse must never reroll or reapply it. Founders remain exactly two and immortal, ordinary world viability and resource rules still apply. Examples (not required catalog): exposed iron, unusual medicinal plant, natural spring, meteor fragment, lightning-caused fire observation, and mysterious artifact. Alien contact is an illustrative future setting option; if adopted, model it initially as a bounded observation/artifact rather than an alien civilization/space engine. MVP founding catalog must use a small set of already supported mechanics, with richer origins deferred until their prerequisites exist.
A discovery may unlock eligibility or modify weights for specific related discoveries, experiments or encounters through actual retained knowledge, artifacts, conditions, materials, skill and exploration. Examples: meteor fragment enables unusual alloy experimentation; a rare pigment enables distinctive art; astronomy enables recognition of an unusual celestial event. Influence may be positive or negative, conditional, consumed, lost or obsolete; no universal luck stat, unbounded stacking, guaranteed follow-up, self-amplifying probability loop, forced plot or obligation to see every event. Cap aggregate chance by tier and opportunity budget even when many influences apply; maintain strictly positive no-event possibility. Actual discoveries/earned milestones are not throttled or erased to enforce loot rarity. Historical discoveries remain recorded even when their active influence is lost. Catalog may contain mutually exclusive or permanently ineligible paths. Goal: decades of real-time operation still leave meaningful unexplored possibilities; there is no completion screen, minimum collection percentage or guarantee of all encounters even with indefinite operation. Owner approval of numeric rates and influence caps required before release.

## Exceptional milestones must feel exceptional (owner-directed)
Rare, epic and legendary encounters/discoveries must represent substantive world events, not cosmetic loot labels. Catalog admission must identify an actual unusual condition, discovery, loss or opportunity with meaningful potential consequences, affected location/people and causal provenance. A rare event may materially affect an individual, family or settlement; epic events may affect several settlements or an important capability; legendary events offer genuinely civilization-defining potential. Rarity does not guarantee success, positive consequences or a global permanent bonus. A failed experiment, destroyed artifact or forgotten discovery may still be a historically significant event. Classify earned achievements by actual exceptional accomplishment; do not randomly promote ordinary events to legendary.
At discovery/occurrence, present a distinct tier-appropriate announcement on the display, original visual emphasis and optional brief sound if hardware supports it. Rare: prominent nonblocking announcement; epic: enhanced emphasis and optional map highlight; legendary: unmistakable special presentation and permanent featured chronicle entry. Genesis event receives its own unique founding presentation. No forced camera interruption of inspection, mandatory acknowledgement, blocking animation, sound during quiet mode, or simulation pause; renderer consumes read-only event records and never draws simulation RNG. Queue bounded announcements, preserve all durable event records, and allow observers to replay/inspect missed events without replaying their effects. Show actual date, discoverer or affected citizens if known, location, what happened and observed consequences; distinguish possible future implications from facts and avoid omniscient spoilers for hidden deposits. Link later consequences by causal event IDs; do not award repeated rarity announcements for the same milestone on reboot. No claim that a rare event must improve civilization.
Initial MVP: one shared milestone presentation and journal path with tier-specific styling and original lightweight effects, subject to verified hardware/audio support. Rich animations, procedural fanfares and elaborate cinematic sequences are optional enhancements. Test unique event IDs, exactly-once logical milestone recording, notification isolation, read-only replay, missed-event retrieval, quiet mode, queue pressure and uninterrupted simulation. Preserve decades-scale rarity calibration and Genesis exception.

## Consequential world effects (design extension)
Founding events, rare encounters, disasters, discoveries and exceptional achievements may confer beneficial and adverse effects through a shared deterministic modifier/effect system. Effects must represent actual causes: a spring supplies water, an improved tool saves labor, a plague harms health, an archive preserves knowledge. Scope may be a citizen, household, settlement, region or genuinely world-wide condition; no blanket global luck or advancement bonus merely because a milestone has a rarity label. Mixed outcomes and indirect trade-offs are allowed. Distinguish one-time transactions (stock or health changes) from persistent conditional capabilities and temporary modifiers. Effects may expire, be consumed, deteriorate, be counteracted, or end when their source or required knowledge is lost. Persistent geographic conditions last while physically present; permanent history does not imply permanent active buffs. Apply benefits only to relevant supported mechanics, with bounded fixed-point arithmetic, stable resolution order and explicit stacking caps; never bypass founder immortality, resource conservation, eligibility, capacity or aggregate encounter pacing. Preserve an explicit no-event outcome. No forced success, irreversible civilization-wide punishment from a single random roll, or artificial rescue. UI inspection should explain source, scope, active effects, expiry/conditions and observed consequences without revealing hidden discoveries. MVP implement a small number of reusable effects through existing health, ecology, work and knowledge primitives as these become available; advanced catalogs are staged. Validate positive and negative effects, combined caps, loss/expiration, geographic scope, exactly-once transactions, replay, and unchanged simulation under UI inspection.
