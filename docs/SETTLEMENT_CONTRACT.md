# Disposable settlement return
The completed shelter is an initial shared home anchor for the founding pair, not an automatically founded town. Derive anchor from the actual structure tile, with no extra map or canonical citizen fields. Prototype state records cumulative purposeful homeward steps and arrivals. A founder may return only during a discretionary action after urgent survival, care, reunion and research/construction/storage reservations. No active construction or experiment is displaced. Healthy enough to travel (health >20), hunger and thirst <=8, within Manhattan 8 of home, home tile has at least 12 food and 12 water units. If needs are urgent or the site is depleted/unbuilt/remote, the ordinary survival and exploration policy remains in charge. Return uses the same single cardinal step and Action::move as ordinary travel. Prefer return when recovering (health <90) or during the first 12 ticks of each 120-tick window; at home do not pin the citizen there. This is a provisional lightweight visit cadence, not a day/night clock, permanent residency or guaranteed settlement. No teleport, no new RNG, no forced births or discoveries. Infants still follow actual parents. Future multiple homes, occupancy, construction placement and farming demand require separate work.
Policy is composed above the existing default storage work policy; no change to old probes or saves. Separate Elecrow settlement probe extends shelter/cooking/storage and reports actual return steps and arrivals. Host tests exercise distance, scarcity, knowledge-independent shelter use, survival priority, active work and ordinary movement; report actual visits in an integrated seed-42 smoke, without requiring every world to settle. Recovery benefits remain limited by actual completed shelter and ordinary health rules.
