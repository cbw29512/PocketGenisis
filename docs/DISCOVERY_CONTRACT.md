# Disposable early discovery increment
Owner approved higher ordinary experiment success rates; keep one tick/second and 43200 ticks/year. Initial catalog: fire, stone tools (70% each); shelter (50%), cooking (50%), food storage (50%). These are prototype concept discoveries only; effects/building deployment, hunting, fishing, agriculture, domestication, ore, metallurgy and later technologies require their respective real mechanics. Do not fake metalworking with stone stock. Rare encounter RNG/budgets untouched.
## State
Separate disposable ResearchState: two founder knowledge bitmasks, first discovery record per catalog entry (tick, founder ID), one active experiment (owner slot, catalog ID, accumulated work), cumulative attempts/successes/failures, last opportunity tick. No new World fields or production save changes. Founder knowledge is permanent and may be taught locally. First-discovery history does not confer knowledge automatically. Only existing research RNG used for candidate selection and outcome, no reseeding. Stable catalog indices, integer percentage draws; no floating point. 
## Rules
After a valid human tick, an adult founder with health >=50, both needs <=24 and last action idle may contribute one experiment work unit. A new experiment can begin at most once per cadence boundary (normal candidate 43200 ticks, disposable accelerated probe 60 ticks), if an idle eligible founder has unknown technology with prerequisites known and enough actual local wood/stone/food. Choose uniformly among eligible founder/technology pairs in stable order using research RNG. Deduct costs immediately, including for eventual failure. Active experiment retains owner; progress pauses during urgent survival. Completion after 30 eligible idle work ticks; success roll 0..99 against catalog base probability plus bounded curiosity/creativity modifier (-5..+5 points), within approved ranges. Failed attempts do not unlock; retry requires a fresh opportunity and materials. Teaching after successful tick: once per 60 ticks, if founders within Manhattan 2 and both eligible, transfer at most one known technology to the other, prerequisites required. No duplicate first discovery. No hidden global era unlock, no time-based guaranteed discovery, no modification to other RNG streams. At most one active experiment; no new per-citizen scans. Prototype founder-only; descendants, durable research/teaching history, save/reload, construction and capability-dependent eras are future requirements.

No official founding. Multi-seed full progression calibration remains required.
