# Disposable demand-driven housing
The initial three structure slots remain the original blueprints. Only PG_HOUSING_PROBE builds add eight additional shelter slots (nine shelters total), retaining the same Site fields, hut blueprint, existing material debit, work progression and abandonment rules. Site index identifies a physical site; blueprint kind is derived (all extra slots are huts). Never index blueprint catalog with a site index. One active construction job as before; no occupied-tile overlap. Same founder knowledge and labor guards, no free completion.
A single founding settlement has provisional planning capacity of two living citizens per completed shelter. Additional shelters are eligible only when a first shelter exists and total living citizens exceed completed shelter capacity. This counts actual living citizens, not anticipated births; it is an initial single-settlement approximation, not individual occupancy or a population cap. Extra hut candidate must be on a currently unoccupied tile within Manhattan 8 of an existing completed shelter and have at least 12 food and 12 water plus the existing material costs. No forced relocation, new random draws, or birth changes. Stable existing site/founder order. Abandoned sites stay recorded, and an exhausted prototype catalog reports its limit instead of overwriting structures.
Home visits select the nearest viable completed shelter within existing return radius, ties by site order; a citizen on a viable shelter is not pulled to another. Recovery slots are per completed shelter (two per tick), with unchanged natural recovery rules. No ownership assignment or automatic claim of permanent residence. Earlier builds have exactly three site slots and unchanged mechanics. Do not link differently configured State definitions together. Future production needs dynamic or appropriately bounded spatial housing, local demand and persistent records, without compiling a new engine per building.
Verification: controlled host tests cover unmet demand, materials, viability, local expansion, shared blueprint progress, occupancy, no overbuilding, nearest-home selection and per-shelter recovery. Separate one-year Elecrow housing probe reports real births, completed shelters, planning capacity and unmet demand. Actual construction may be absent if a seed's founders lack opportunities; do not force a birth or building. No canonical save, renderer, terrain or Tab5 changes.
