# Wildlife — approved extension, disposable first slice
Owner approved animals, hunting, fishing, domestication and rebenchmarking on 2026-11-27. Human-only benchmarks remain unchanged. The first slice is a disposable sidecar state, not a new production save format.
## Data and invariants
One WildlifeTile per existing map tile, seven uint16 counts (deer, rabbit, boar, wild cattle, wild chicken, wolf, bear) and uint16 edible meat units. Counts bounded at 64 per species per tile as a provisional prototype representation limit; no silent truncation or forced reseeding. Meat bounded at 100 by harvesting admission; no kill if insufficient storage. Independent seeded placement; no draws from existing human streams. All integer operations and row/species order stable. Tile.food represents edible vegetation in this experiment; water and food stocks are shared with citizens. Sidecar state is explicitly initialized for each disposable benchmark and not serialized. Never enable in an official world until canonical persistence and replay are implemented.
## Tick rules
After successful authoritative human tick, idle healthy adults with hunger >=24 may consume one local meat unit (12 hunger relief) or harvest one local prey animal into a species-specific 2–8 meat units, consuming one immediately. No double human action, no remote hunting or created animal. No hunting prerequisite or hunting risk yet; this is a prototype transaction, not final hunting AI. Each 60 ticks, terrestrial herbivore/omnivore groups consume one food and water per surviving member; unfed members die. Wolves and bears consume local prey and water; bears may consume vegetation if prey unavailable. Every 600 ticks, surviving groups of at least two can add a bounded fraction of their count (minimum one), consuming additional food/water or prey; no resurrection from zero. Each wildlife pulse spoils one stored meat unit per tile. All coefficients provisional load-test parameters, not biological rates. No migration, ages, individual animal identities, interspecies disease, direct predator attacks on humans or livestock yet. Food conversion is abstract, not calibrated mass or energy balance.
## Acceptance and subsequent stages
Host tests: shared stocks never underflow, harvest consumes a real animal, no free meat, starvation/extinction, predator-prey accounting, reproduction and deterministic state. Device probe: 2048 and 4096 ordinary/crowded humans, 600 ticks each, separately report wildlife peak and combined max, actual starting/ending animals, hunting and deaths; same baseline environment remains available. Wildlife state allocated in PSRAM separately; no added task-stack arrays. Target combined tick <=200ms with safe headroom; physical runs required. Next: tune long-horizon food web; spatial migration, water terrain and fish; learned hunting/fishing/cooking; domestication, managed breeding, livestock products; animals' age/health/disease and human interactions; durable ecology state and history, explicit versioned save/replay and rendering. Preserve finite world and natural carrying capacity; no hidden global population death multiplier.
