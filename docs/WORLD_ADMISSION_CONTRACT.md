# PX-33 — Balanced procedural creation
Owner wants random worlds with complete starting ecological and technological opportunity, not worlds doomed by absent essential resources. Distinguish starting qualification from ongoing ecological rules. Resources are initially distributed unevenly, with redundant habitable areas; after creation, genuine depletion, migration, loss, recovery and extinction remain possible. Do not use admission to rescue a running world. No guaranteed prosperity or permanent resource floors.
Optional host candidate world_admission.hpp reuses existing terrain, finite geology, wildlife and fish initialization, not a second engine. Read-only report counts actual forest/grassland tiles, mineral amounts, provisioned breeding-size wildlife groups, water and fish by quadrant, and viable adjacent founding pairs. Provisional thresholds: forest/grassland each >=20; all eight modeled minerals each >=8 units; >=2 provisioned breeding-size groups per species; >=16 inhabited fish tiles and >=2 quadrants with >=8 fish tiles; >=2 quadrants with >=8 water-stock tiles; at least one adjacent non-dry/non-highland founder pair with food/water>=40 wood>=20 stone>=12. Seed-domain reservoir chooses a pair without touching live RNG. This is not verified hydrological redundancy, actual distinct river systems, predator habitat qualification, full agricultural suitability or long-term sufficiency. Exact thresholds provisional and require calibrated capacity/resource budgets for eventual technology, including deeper deposits. No new persistent per-tick memory/loops.
