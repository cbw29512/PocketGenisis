# Disposable shelter recovery increment
A completed shelter provides a local protected recovery opportunity, not free healing from starvation, dehydration, disease or aging. Existing health logic runs first. If it naturally increases a citizen's health, and the citizen is idle on the actual completed shelter tile, still below 100 health, grant one additional point (at most two total recovery points that tick). At most two extra recovery grants per world tick, in the existing stable founder/descendant order. These are recovery slots, not permanent residence assignments or a limit on all visitors. No resurrection, damage immunity, remote benefit, fabricated supplies, or change to mortality or fertility rules. Benefit is passive for stationary recovery; existing idle work representation does not distinguish rest from stationary research. Do not claim an explicit sleep/rest simulation. State: cumulative extra recovery grants, no extra canonical World fields. Capacity and health bonus provisional. No weather model, occupancy assignments, decay, return-to-shelter behavior or new saves in this increment.
Implement optional recovery callback at the authoritative mortality phase, invoked only after existing ordinary health increases, before death/age checks. Default no-op preserves existing rules and old probes. Shelter probe extends cooking and storage and runs a disposable seed42 one-year simulation. Report actual bonus grants; zero is legitimate when no injured citizens are idle at shelter. Controlled host tests verify conditions, slots, cap, starvation, disease, dead citizens and natural old-age death. Future settlement planning must make buildings accessible, expand capacity as demand grows, and avoid forcing citizens to remain in depleted areas.
