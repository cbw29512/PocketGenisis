# Pixels — autonomous needs and economic progression (owner direction 2026-11-27)
Public terminology: a person in Pocket Genesis is a Pixel; plural Pixels. The two original immortal Pixels are the Founders. Preserve established Citizen type, IDs, history and serialization compatibility; no mechanical rename or forced migration. Use Pixels in observer UI and documentation when describing people.
 
The owner wants Pixels to have a reason to travel, gather varied resources, hunt, farm and pursue further economic activities. Resource abundance and local shortages must create meaningful choices, not lock agents into scripted paths or make all tiles identical. Keep the fixed 100x60 world, bounded work, physical travel, distinct personalities, independent seeded emergence and real resource accounting. No universal era timer, invisible global unlock, guaranteed hunt or forced population quota.
 
## Progression and acceptance
- Early Pixels explore and gather edible plants, water, wood and stone according to local need/opportunity. Travel has a real cost in time, exposure, hunger and thirst. Do not make every tile equally productive after terrain integration; keep recoverable renewable stocks and viable founder recovery.
- Hunting must be a real survival/food choice before farming is discovered. Inspect current wildlife::hunt and survival: existing post-survival hunting requires idle adult hunger>=24 but survival already acts at >=12. This explains why integrated diagnostics can have zero hunts even with wildlife. Integrate actual local meat and hunt as a bounded need-driven food option within authoritative survival; use existing animal count, meat yield, storage/spoilage and satisfaction primitives. Prefer existing meat to killing additional animals, forbid free meat, no killing on developed land, no remote kill, ordinary travel toward actual known/local prey or food. Keep infant care and urgent hydration first. Different terrain/resources and prey availability should affect routes. Controlled and unforced tests must report actual hunts/meals, resource and wildlife conservation, no double action, same-seed replay and effects on species populations.
- Learned farming, irrigation, domestication, fishing and storage create alternative, complementary and more dependable food sources as settlement demand grows. Hunting remains possible afterward; no global prohibition or guaranteed transition. Farming should not be an automatic upgrade merely because food is scarce. Require actual knowledge, materials, seed, water, site, labor and useful output. Fishing depends on real aquatic habitat/population. Herd feeding and winter planning remain resource-constrained.
- Later professions, mining, pottery, trade and further technology should solve real local material, health, transport, production or knowledge needs. Do not invent output without input or labor. Record meaningful historical discoveries and actions with stable Pixel IDs. 
- Compare diverse seeds and meaningful behaviors, not just total population. Track food-source meals, hunts, capture/breeding, harvests, local shortages, travel, seasonal stock, species extinctions, resource debits, health, births/deaths and historical milestones. No arbitrary target counts or forced progression; provisional rates need calibration. Include long-run and device evidence before beta.
 
Immediate order: repair failed construction recovery regression without weakening population validation; then authoritative survival hunting and bounded food-source search, followed by measured coexistence of hunting/farming/livestock, terrain and fishing. No external code necessary for reuse of existing food/animal/movement primitives. No beta installation or official founding.
