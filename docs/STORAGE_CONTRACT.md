# Disposable functional food store
The completed food-store blueprint becomes a local reserve, not a source of free food. Separate prototype state: stored units (0..64), lifetime deposits, withdrawals, spoilage. One existing store only; no canonical World or save changes. One unit is the same food unit consumed by the existing survival engine.
A healthy idle founder within Manhattan 1 of a completed store may deposit one unit from their current tile when local food is greater than 24 and reserve is below 64. Deposits use a discretionary action, after survival, care, research and construction. Store work can reserve a free action instead of wandering, but never interrupts an active project. No long-distance transport. A citizen on or adjacent to the completed store whose primary urgent need is food may consume one stored unit if local natural food consumption fails, before searching distant food. This applies to independently acting citizens, including eligible descendants; dependent feeding remains on the existing care path for now. Existing hunger reduction and Action::eat semantics reused. No double consumption. One stored unit spoils every 3600 ticks if any exists, including when unused. No invented food, bonus health or global modifiers. Deposits = withdrawals + spoilage + balance (initial zero). Stable order; no new RNG. No automatic settlement return, other building benefits or persistent store inventory yet.
Verification: controlled unit tests prove material transfer, local scarcity consumption, distance/completion/capacity guards, spoilage and conservation, with integrated seeded tick smoke test. Separate device build runs a normal one-year disposable civilization with accelerated discoveries; reports reserve activity and births, no forced shortages. Zero withdrawals in a plentiful world is not a failure; controlled shortage tests verify that path. Default production, discovery and family probes retain previous rules. Larger multi-seed and durable persistence remain gates.

## Shared dependent reserve consumption

Debit at most one real reserve unit per successful meal, apply existing hunger/action semantics, and count assisted withdrawals as a subset of withdrawals. Provider must be within one tile of completed store and recipient within one tile of provider; use existing caregiver eligibility and no second feeding. Primary water need cannot be satisfied with food; existing secondary food opportunity may draw a reserve unit only after its natural food attempt fails. Default no-reserve policy preserves previous canonical rules. Added controlled tests for infant care, founder assistance, duplicate/distance/water guards and secondary hunger.
