# Disposable functional campfire
A cooked meal is one actual raw food or reserve unit plus one actual wood unit from a completed campfire tile. The provider must personally know cooking (currently founder knowledge only), be within Manhattan 1 of that fire, and be within Manhattan 1 of the recipient. After the shared successful food transaction, reduce remaining hunger by at most six additional points (normal food relief 12; cooked maximum 18), only if positive hunger remains. No fuel spent if the meal would have no extra benefit. No bonus for water, unavailable food, unbuilt fire, unknown cooking, absent fuel or distant provider. The meal uses the existing eating or care action, no double action, no food generation. Track meals, wood spent and extra hunger relief; one fuel per cooked meal. Fuel cost and relief provisional, not historical calibration. No persistent fields, separate heat/weather effects, travel-to-fire logic or automatic knowledge for descendants. Existing no-op-by-default policy preserves canonical and earlier probe behavior.
Tests: ordinary food and stored food, assisted feeding, unknown knowledge, no fuel, no food, water and distance guards, conservation and deterministic integrated smoke. Separate Elecrow cooking probe extends storage. Normal local meals may not need extra relief and founders may leave the fire: zero cooked meals on the unforced hardware seed is reported, not silently forced. Controlled host fixtures demonstrate effects. Real shelter and settlement decisions remain subsequent gates.
