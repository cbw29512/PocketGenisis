# PB-01 wildlife cadence calibration, first candidate
Evidence: combined livestock smoke at 9e6e767 and 9a3d90b built a pen, produced 41 crop harvests, ended with 21 wild animals and zero captures. Existing shared vegetation renewal is 0–2 food units per tile per 60 ticks and 1–3 water units; a group of four grazers alone demands four of each every 60 ticks, before other consumers, and winter provides no food regeneration. This is a concrete mismatch in local demand rates, not proof it is the sole cause of population decline.
Provisional candidate: keep migration, hunting, displacement and meat spoilage cadence unchanged. Feed all wild/managed groups every 600 ticks; evaluate reproduction every 3600 ticks after actual feeding, using the same resource-debit and birth/death rules. No change to stock caps, human renewal, initial animals, random streams or population targets; no minimum population, artificial resource injection, scripted breeding or rescue. Original wildlife benchmarks and standalone controlled transactions retain original cadence. Combined civilization uses candidate; changes in timing affect simulated outcomes and must be versioned when integrated. Cadence constraints: positive feeding multiple of 60; breeding positive multiple of feeding. Local scarcity and species extinction still possible.
Host comparative test uses fixed seeds 0 and 42 under both cadences, actual human/seasonal tick, reports per-quarter species counts, consumption and causes; population conservation required, outcomes observed rather than tuned to a desired count. Controlled test proves delayed feeding, unchanged spoilage, actual resource-funded births and starvation/extinction; combined test adds initial, births, deaths, predation, hunting, displacement and resource/managed counters. New CI pending. No claim of ecological sustainability, autonomous capture, long-horizon acceptance or device performance until measured. Broader seed/years, fishing, grazing competition, species-specific behavior and purposeful capture still pending. No external code or official state changes.
