# Disposable agriculture increment
Only PG_AGRICULTURE_PROBE extends the discovery catalog with Farming (stone tools + food storage prerequisites, costs 2 wood, 1 stone, 4 food, 35% base success, same experiment/teaching rules). The existing structure catalog gains one prepared field blueprint (farming knowledge, 2 wood, 1 stone, 20 work ticks). A field may be established on an unoccupied local tile within Manhattan 8 of a completed shelter, with food >=14, water >=12, and either living population >2 or local food <=48. One field is a disposable prototype limit, not a production farm limit. Housing behavior remains available. Construction and fields share occupancy; no simultaneous structure on a field. Earlier profiles retain their catalog and layout.
Field state: growth stage 0 empty, 1 planted, 2..4 watered/tended; last work tick; cumulative plants, tending actions, water spent, harvests and food produced. Only an available founder personally knowing farming may work the completed field, on its tile. Planting costs 2 actual food seeds and 1 water, leaving at least 12 local food; do not plant if food exceeds 92. Three subsequent tending actions consume 1 water each, separated by >=120 ticks. Harvest after a further >=120 ticks, adds exactly 8 food to field tile only if capacity permits (natural cap 100). No partial harvest or silent truncation. Plant/tend pause in winter; ripe harvest allowed. Crop can wait indefinitely in this prototype; disease, spoilage, drought losses, soil and irrigation are not modeled yet. Successful harvest contributes to reported tick food production. No free food from mere discovery or structure completion.
Use existing single cardinal movement for a nearby eligible farmer traveling to a ready task; never teleport. Only discretionary actions after survival, care, founder reunion and research/construction/storage reservation. Farm work is committed after the authoritative tick only if the reserved farmer is still eligible/on field and no research, construction or deposit occurred that tick. At most one farm task per tick. If busy, allow ordinary behavior; no forced population, discovery or harvest. Counters enforce seed and water accounting. No new RNG streams or canonical saved fields. Numeric costs/yields/cadence provisional and must be calibrated. 
Verification: controlled tests demonstrate knowledge/prerequisite, material/land/demand, actual planting, time/water/season/capacity guards, harvest accounting and work reservation; one-year seed42 integrated smoke records observed results without forcing successful agriculture. Separate Elecrow agriculture probe logs real farm metrics. Older profiles unchanged. No official save/rendering/Tab5 changes.
