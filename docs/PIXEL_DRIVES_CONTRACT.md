# Pixel drives and environmental feedback — owner direction 2026-11-27
Design approved in principle; numerical tuning and runtime implementation pending. Reuse existing hunger/thirst, health, traits, family eligibility, crowding/disease/resource stress, seeded decisions and local movement. Do not replace the entire behavior engine or duplicate existing needs. Existing Citizen IDs and canonical save remain unchanged until a tested schema migration is approved.

## State and decision contract
- Existing byte hunger/thirst and separate health remain authoritative. Candidate additional persistent uint8 0..100 drives: fatigue, reproductive motivation, social need, curiosity, comfort and ambition; safety/stress may be persistent only if a demonstrated need for memory exists, otherwise derived from local risk. Names/ranges provisional; distinguish durable personal motivation from transient environmental context. Personality influences growth, sensitivity and satisfaction, not guaranteed decisions.
- Derive bounded local context from actual neighboring population, available housing, food/water, terrain, predators, weather, known opportunities and events. Cache spatial aggregates at deterministic tick boundaries; avoid per-Pixel global population scans and duplicate map state. No renderer or wall-clock effects. Use integer/fixed-point arithmetic, stable tie ordering, seeded variation only through documented streams.
- A Pixel weighs eligible actions using its needs, traits, travel cost, risk, learned abilities and actual local resources. Survival and dependent care retain precedence; no automatic behavior from a high drive without valid action prerequisites. Actions satisfy drives only after actual work/consumption/rest/interaction. Saturate safely, no negative resources or invented outputs.

## Feedback examples and limits
- Sparse local settlement or rare compatible adult encounters can increase reproductive motivation and desire for companionship over time, especially when health, food, shelter and care capacity are adequate. A recovering founder pair can receive bounded need pressure, never bypass ordinary conception checks. No world population target, global fertility switch, guaranteed pregnancy, emergency spawning, forced pair formation or reproduction of minors. Retain current compatible-pair, gestation, health, age, resource, kinship and capacity constraints. Scarcity/illness may reduce actual reproductive viability even with strong drive. Account for gestation and care delays to avoid runaway growth.
- Crowding can satiate social need while increasing crowding stress and desire for personal space/exploration; avoid conflating loneliness and crowding into one sign-flipped value. Use local density relative to housing/resources and individual tolerance, not a universal city-size penalty. Offer viable nearby or known destinations; travel uses real steps and costs. Preserve social bonds, ordinary jobs, family care and settlement benefits. No compulsory exodus or forced death.
- Under threat, increased safety pressure motivates shelter/avoidance; winter/cold increases warmth, storage and rest needs; scarcity increases food seeking and experimentation incentives; abundant resources free time for creativity, cooperation, trade and ambition. An actual disaster or disease changes local conditions, not a global luck modifier. Recovery and rebuilding can reduce stress. Events may alter effective action scores or bounded persistent state, not rewrite every personality.
- Use gradual accumulation, bounded responses and recovery/decay (hysteresis if needed) to avoid flip-flopping between actions or mass simultaneous migration. Maintain exploration opportunities for different personalities. No fixed global population equilibrium or scripted history.

## Verification
Controlled low-density vs crowded, safe vs dangerous, hungry vs fed and winter vs summer comparisons; eligible/ineligible reproductive and travel cases; no overflows, forced reproduction, population caps masquerading as motivation or unsupported new action. Demonstrate behavior diversity under same circumstances, same-seed replay, multi-seed trajectories, local recovery/settlement expansion, conserved resources, realistic travel and no persistent oscillation or deadlock. Measure state size, bounded spatial lookup/tick cost on Elecrow, and persistence/migration before release. Existing founder recovery and crowding rules reused. Extend incrementally after construction test fix and authoritative hunting; do not bypass PB-01. No beta/official founding until release gates.

## PX-03 fatigue candidate
Optional PG_FATIGUE_PROBE, shared survival action path for independently acting citizens. Two logical uint8 fields fatigue0..100, rest_intent0/1; appended Action::rest without renumbering existing actions. Every8 ticks staggered by stable ID fatigue+1 capped100, threshold72 sets intent; actual rest action recovers4 until<=24, then clears intent. Hunger/thirst and dependent care remain first, and emergency founder assistance precedes rest. Interrupted rest retains intent; no restoration without actual rest. Idle-only work/research/construction checks reject resting actors. Infants remain on existing dependent care path. No RNG, per-Pixel searches or extra map scans. No fatigue damage, trait/shelter modifiers or canonical serialization/rendering yet; numerical thresholds provisional. Test actual actions, priority, invalid state, same-seed combined annual runs with terrain/weather/fish/soil/manure; CI pending. Physical size/padding and integrated target timing still require measurement. Keep nonprobe behavior unchanged. Do not flash permanent world.
